Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. ⚠ Review details on third-country transfers and data processing agreements.

## 3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking services and does not embed third-party fonts or content. Fonts are served from this server.

## 4. Contacting us

The enquiry form does not transmit any data to us or third parties. Submitting it opens your email client with a prepared message. If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to handle the enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer required and no statutory retention obligations apply.

## 5. Applications

We process applications sent by email to carry out the application process (§ 26 BDSG, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). ⚠ Define and add the retention period.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
